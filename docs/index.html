How it's ranked

Highest rated first, with a few rules so a list never gets padded or gamed.

01Real vote counts

Every film needs at least 1,000 IMDb votes, and more in busy modern years, so a handful of fans can't crown a winner.

02Weighted like the Top 250

Films with fewer votes are nudged towards their year's average until there's enough evidence they belong at the top.

03No filler

Nothing rated under 6.0 makes it. Lists run up to 250 films. Older years are shorter because fewer films clear the bar.

04Feature films only

No documentaries, shorts or TV. Refreshed weekly as new votes roll in.

What this is

A free, unofficial Stremio add-on for walking through film history one year at a time. Stremio's own year view shows what came out, newest first. This shows what was worth watching.

It adds catalog lists only: titles, posters and details. It doesn't host or provide any streams.
